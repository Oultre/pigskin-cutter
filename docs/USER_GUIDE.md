# Pigskin Cutter — Coach's Guide

A friendly walkthrough for getting clips out of your game film. No computer
background needed. You can't break anything by clicking around, so explore freely.

> The same guide is built into the app — click the **Help** tab up top any time.

---

## What this does

You give it game film and a list of plays. It hands you back short clips of just
the plays you want — 3rd & longs, red zone, a certain formation, whatever — so you
never scrub through hours of tape again.

Everything runs on your own computer. Your film is never uploaded anywhere, and
your original film is never changed — the app only makes new clip files.

---

## Opening the app

Usually whoever set this up gets it running for you, and you open it in your web
browser (like Chrome). You'll see three or four tabs across the top:

- **Plays** — find plays and make clips. You'll live here.
- **Films** — the games loaded in.
- **Tag pass** — mark up a game yourself.
- **Help** — this guide.

---

## 1. Finding the plays you want

On the **Plays** tab, the left column is your search — think of it like the
filters on a Hudl breakdown.

1. Click **+ condition** and pick a rule, e.g. *down is 3*.
2. Add another for *distance is 6 or more*.
3. Optionally limit it to one game, or to only the plays a person confirmed.
4. Click **Apply**. The matching plays fill the middle of the screen.

Each play remembers where it came from. Plays a computer guessed show a faded
confidence number — tick **confirmed only** to leave those out.

## 2. Presets — your saved searches

Made a search you'll reuse? Save it once, click it forever.

- Type a name under "save current filter as…" and hit **Save**.
- Re-run it any time from the **load preset** dropdown.
- **Import** / **Export** share a set of searches with another coach or move them
  to your other computer.

## 3. Watching a play and fixing the trim

Click any play in the middle list to open it on the right.

- The video jumps to that play — play it, scrub it, look it over.
- If a clip starts a touch too early or late, nudge the **start** / **end** with
  the −0.5 / −0.1 / +0.1 / +0.5 buttons, or hit **= playhead** to snap the point
  to wherever the video is paused.
- Changes save automatically.

## 4. Making your clips

In the **Export** box on the left:

1. Enter an **output folder** — where the clips get saved on your computer.
2. Optional: add a **logo** image to stamp your school's mark on each clip.
   (Branded clips take a little longer, since each one is re-made.)
3. Click **Dry run** first — it shows exactly what it will make, without writing
   anything.
4. Happy? Click **Cut clips**. They land in your folder.

Your original film is never touched — clips are always brand-new files.

## 5. Tagging a game yourself (Tag pass)

No breakdown for a game? Watch it once and mark the plays. Pick the film at the
top of the **Tag pass** tab, then use a keyboard **or** an Xbox-style controller:

| Keyboard | Does |
|---|---|
| `Space` | play / pause |
| `←` `→` | back / forward 1 second (hold `Shift` for 5) |
| `,` `.` | step one frame |
| `I` / `O` | mark the start / end of a play |
| `1`–`4` | set the down |
| `L` `M` `R` | set the hash |
| `Enter` | save the play and move on |
| `Z` | undo the last play |
| `Esc` | clear |

**Controller:** A = mark start, B = mark end, X = save, Y = clear, bumpers scrub,
D-pad sets the down, Start plays/pauses. A little "gamepad ✓" appears when a
controller is plugged in.

Fill in distance / formation / etc. as you go — they save with the play.

## 6. Films and play-by-play

On the **Films** tab you can add a game and see what's loaded. For a TV-broadcast
game, the **Import play-by-play** box pulls the official play list from the
athletics website — paste the game's box-score link and it fills in every play's
down, distance, and result for you.

If the setup here looks unfamiliar, no worries — loading film is usually handled
by whoever set the app up. Your part is the fun part: finding and cutting plays.

## Common questions

**The play list is empty.**
Click **Apply** in the search — with no conditions it shows every play.

**A clip won't export.**
A play with no start/end time can't be cut yet (it needs trimming or a tag pass).
Those are skipped, and the app tells you how many.

**Everything looks broken, or it says "Failed to fetch".**
The app's engine probably isn't running. Ask whoever set it up to restart it —
nothing you did caused it.

**Can I hurt my film?**
No. The app only reads your film and makes new clips. Originals are safe.

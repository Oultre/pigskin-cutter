# Pigskin Cutter — Coach's Guide

A friendly walkthrough for getting clips out of your game film. No computer
background needed. You can't break anything by clicking around, so explore freely.

> The same guide is built into the app — from the home screen, click
> **Coach's guide** at the bottom.

---

## What this does

You give it game film and a list of plays. It hands you back short clips of just
the plays you want — 3rd & longs, red zone, a certain formation, whatever — so you
never scrub through hours of tape again.

Everything runs on your own computer. Your film is never uploaded anywhere, and
your original film is never changed — the app only makes new clip files.

---

## The home screen: pick what you're doing

When the app opens you get five big cards. Click one to start; the logo up top
always brings you back home.

- **Clip Cutter** — find plays and make clips. This is where you'll spend most of your time.
- **Auto Detect** — let the app find the plays for you.
- **Data Grab** — pull the official play-by-play for a college game.
- **Build Reel** — string a bunch of plays into one highlight video.
- **Film Library** — add a game and see what's loaded.

Underneath those, two more links: **Tag a game by hand** and **Coach's guide**.

Up in the top corner are a **light/dark switch** and a **gear** for Settings.

---

## 1. Clip Cutter — finding the plays you want

Every play shows as a little thumbnail, like a contact sheet.

Along the top is a row of **Suggested cuts** — one-click filters like *3rd & Long*,
*Explosive*, *Touchdowns*, *Pass* / *Run*. Click one and the grid narrows to just
those plays, with a count.

Want something more specific?

1. Click **Advanced filter**.
2. Add a rule, e.g. *down is 3*.
3. Add another for *distance is 7 or more*.
4. Click **Apply**.

Made a search you'll reuse? **Save as preset** puts it in your own row of suggested
cuts. Presets can be imported and exported to share with another coach or move to
your other computer.

Each play remembers where it came from. A play the computer guessed carries an
**OCR** or **CHECK** badge instead of **MATCHED** — tick **confirmed only** in the
filter to leave the guesses out. If you've run the alignment check (below), the
**✓ Verified only** chip shows just the plays whose down & distance were confirmed
against the video.

## 2. Watching a play and fixing the trim

Click any play in the grid to open it on the right.

- The video jumps to that play — play it, scrub it, look it over.
- If a clip starts a touch too early or late, nudge the **start** / **end** with the
  −0.5 / −0.1 / +0.1 / +0.5 buttons, or hit **= here** to snap the point to wherever
  the video is paused.
- Changes save automatically.

You can also drive this panel with a game controller — see §7.

## 3. Making your clips (and sizing them for social)

In the **Export clips** box on the left:

1. Enter an **output folder** — where the clips get saved on your computer. (Set a
   default once in Settings and it fills in for you.)
2. Pick a **size**. Leave it on *Original* for Hudl, or choose a social size:
   widescreen for YouTube/Hudl/X, square or 4:5 for an Instagram feed, or
   **Vertical** for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and Snapchat.
3. Optional: add a **logo** image to stamp your school's mark on each clip.
   (Branded clips take a little longer, since each one is re-made.)
4. Click **Dry run** first — it shows exactly what it will make, without writing
   anything.
5. Happy? Click **Cut clips**. They land in your folder.

Your original film is never touched — clips are always brand-new files.

## 4. Build Reel — one highlight video

In Clip Cutter, click the little circle on each play you want (a checkmark appears),
then hit **Build reel from selection**. Give it a title, pick a size (go **Vertical**
for a phone), and it stitches those plays into a single video with the down & distance
burned onto each one. It saves into a **reels** folder inside your library.

## 5. Auto Detect — let the app find the plays

Three boxes; which one you use depends on your film.

**Broadcast — read the game clock.** For a TV-broadcast game, this reads the on-screen
game clock and lines your imported play-by-play up with the video, so you don't tag
anything. It needs the play-by-play imported first (see Data Grab) and a visible clock
in the picture. Takes several minutes.

**All-22 & coaches film — detect scene cuts.** On All-22 and end-zone film every play
is its own camera cut, so no game clock is needed — this splits the film at those cuts
into individual plays. If it misses cuts, set **sensitivity** to high; if it finds too
many, set it to low.

**Check the alignment against the video.** Auto-align places plays *approximately*.
This reads each placed play's down & distance back off the video and compares it to the
play-by-play, marking every play **Verified**, **Review**, or **Unchecked**. Run it
after an auto-align, then use **✓ Verified only** in Clip Cutter — cut those with
confidence and eyeball the rest.

## 6. Data Grab — official play-by-play

Pulls the official play list for a game so you don't have to chart it. Type a college's
athletics website (like *minesathletics.com*) and the season, click **Find games**, and
pick your game from the list — it fills in every play's down, distance, and result.

This works for most **college** teams, because their athletics sites all run on the same
platform (Sidearm). It does **not** work for high-school or pro games. If your school
isn't found, you can still paste a box-score link directly.

The plays come in without cut times — run **Auto Detect** to line them up on the video.

## 7. Tagging a game yourself, and using a controller

No breakdown or play-by-play for a game? Watch it once and mark the plays. From the home
screen click **Tag a game by hand** and pick the film.

| Keyboard | Does |
|---|---|
| `Space` | play / pause |
| `←` `→` | back / forward 1 second (hold `Shift` for 5) |
| `,` `.` | step one frame |
| `I` / `O` | mark the start / end of a play |
| `1`–`4` | set the down |
| `L` `M` `R` | set the hash |
| `Enter` | save the play and move on |
| `Z` | undo the last play |
| `Esc` | clear |

Fill in distance / formation / etc. as you go — they save with the play.

### Game controller

An **Xbox** or **PlayStation 5** controller works in two places: **Tag a game by hand**
and the **watch and trim** panel in Clip Cutter. Plug it in (USB or Bluetooth) and
**press any button** — the app can't see a controller until you do, which is why it says
"press a button to connect" until then. Once it wakes up, a **button guide appears on
screen** using the labels for your controller (✕ ○ ▢ △ for PlayStation, A B X Y for
Xbox). With no controller connected, no guide appears.

Tagging (Xbox labels, PlayStation in parentheses):

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| `A` (`✕`) | mark play start |
| `B` (`○`) | mark play end |
| `X` (`▢`) | save the play and move on |
| `Y` (`△`) | clear |
| `LB` / `RB` (`L1` / `R1`) | jump back / forward 1 second |
| D-pad `←` `→` | jump 5 seconds |
| D-pad `↑` `↓` | set the down |
| `Start` (`Options`) | play / pause |
| `Back` (`Create`) | undo the last play |

Watching and trimming in Clip Cutter:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| `Start` (`Options`) | play / pause |
| `LB` / `RB` (`L1` / `R1`) | jump 1 second |
| D-pad `←` `→` | jump 5 seconds |
| `A` (`✕`) | set the start at the playhead |
| `B` (`○`) | set the end at the playhead |
| `X` / `Y` (`▢` / `△`) | trim the tail by a tenth of a second |

Each screen wakes the controller separately — if you switch screens and the guide is
missing, just press a button again.

## 8. Film Library — adding a game

Click **Add film**, then **Browse** to your game file (or drag it onto the window). If
the film lives somewhere else on your computer, the app copies it into your library for
you — you'll see a progress bar. Pick the film type (broadcast, All-22, Hudl, drone) and
click **Add film**.

## 9. Settings

The gear up top, next to the light/dark switch.

- **Where things are saved** — a default folder for clips and one for reels. Set them
  once and they fill in every time; you can still pick something else at export.
- **Your film library** — the folder holding your games and index. **Switch…** opens a
  different library, or point it at an empty folder to start a fresh one.
- **Appearance** — the app starts dark; flip it to light here and it remembers.

## Common questions

**The play grid is empty.**
Click **All plays** at the top of Clip Cutter, or pick a different film from the box in
the top-right.

**A clip won't export.**
A play with no start/end time can't be cut yet (it needs trimming or a tag pass).
Those are skipped, and the app tells you how many.

**Everything looks broken, or it says "Failed to fetch".**
The app's engine probably isn't running. Close it and open it again — nothing you did
caused it.

**Windows says the publisher is unknown when I install.**
Expected. The app isn't code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Can I hurt my film?**
No. The app only reads your film and makes new clips. Originals are safe.
